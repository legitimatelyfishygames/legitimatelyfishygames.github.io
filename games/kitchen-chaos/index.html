The game

A little farm.
A connected world of production.

Kitchen Chaos: Idle Cooking is an idle resource-production and management game for Android. Build resource farms, connect processing stations, and unlock and discover recipes as you develop your production systems.

Upgrade production, progress with in-game currency, and collect manager cards through random manager loot boxes. Local save data and offline progression are part of the game. It remains under development while the first closed-testing build is prepared.

Development & release plans

Two milestones.
Clearly separated.

No public release date announced

01 / First closed test · In preparation

Start with the core game.

  • Core idle gameplay, local saves and offline progression.
  • Managers and random loot boxes obtainable with earned in-game currency.
  • Integrated Google AdMob rewarded ads with Google UMP consent handling and applicable privacy choices.
  • Optional local quest notifications.

The Google Play audience is 18+ only. Closed testing currently targets the UK, Poland, US and New Zealand; future release territories are not fixed by this list.

02 / Future public release · Planned

Checkout awaits testing.

Google Play Billing / Unity IAP is integrated for six diamond packs. Real-money checkout is currently disabled pending product configuration and testing. Purchased diamonds will be usable for random manager chests when payments are enabled.

Product setup, purchase-flow testing and applicable reward-odds disclosures are required before paid purchases are enabled. No public release date or final worldwide territory list is announced.
