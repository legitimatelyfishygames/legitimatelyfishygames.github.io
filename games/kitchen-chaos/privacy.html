1. About this policy

Legitimately Fishy Games is developing Kitchen Chaos: Idle Cooking for Android distribution through Google Play. This draft covers preparation for its first closed test. Older sideloaded prototypes use a different package and separate app data.

The closed-test implementation includes local saves and offline progression, recipes, managers, random manager chests obtained with earned diamonds, optional rewarded advertising and optional local notifications. Basic play does not require watching an ad. No real-money purchases or Google Play Billing are active in this closed test. Future purchases are addressed in section 13.

The handoff reports no developer-operated player account, cloud-save service, custom backend or authored endpoint receiving gameplay saves, and no separately introduced analytics or crash-upload service. Advertising SDK measurement and Android-managed backup are separate. These findings do not mean that no personal information is processed. The studio display name does not imply a registered company; any required legal identity and location disclosures remain under review.

2. Locally stored game data

Readable local JSON saves maintain buildings, layouts and routes; stock, jobs and research; recipes; currencies and spending; sales, skills and ascension; managers and assignments; chest inventory, pity counters and committed random results; goals and claim ledgers; tokens, hints, tutorials; and offline timestamps and timers. Options are stored in a separate local JSON file, including notification preferences.

Local run, chest-opening and ad-attempt identifiers, reward reservations and transaction identifiers help prevent duplicate actions and rewards. The new ad bridge does not send game receipt identifiers as AdMob user identifiers or custom server-verification data. Runtime throughput samples are local presentation data with no authored telemetry sender; notebook search text is transient UI input with no authored persistence or upload found.

The save writer validates and atomically replaces the main save and keeps a recovery .bak. Migrations preserve immutable .before-...json copies, and New Game creates timestamped pre-reset backups. These copies support recovery and can retain historical progress and ledgers. See section 8 for reset and retention limits.

Android backup and device transfer have not been explicitly configured by authored rules in this work. Eligible files and SDK preferences may be included under operating-system defaults, account and device conditions. This is separate from developer cloud saves and is not a guarantee of cross-device recovery. Effective backup and restore behavior still needs device testing. See Android's backup information.

3. Optional local notifications

Quest reminders use Unity Mobile Notifications 2.4.3. They are optional and off by default. Enabling them requests Android notification permission. The implementation forecasts on an isolated local copy of game state, schedules a local quest reminder and cancels its reminder when disabled or when the game resumes. No remote push server, FCM or remote-push token integration was added.

Reminder text can include product names or masked unknown recipes and may appear on the lock screen. Notification permission is separate from advertising consent. Android's app notification settings also provide controls; wording varies by device. Permission denial or revocation, lock-screen display and reminder cancellation still require physical-device testing. See Android's notification permission guidance.

4. Advertising and SDK information

The integration uses Google Mobile Ads Unity 11.6.0 for direct rewarded advertising and its UMP Unity bridge. The handoff reports Google Mobile Ads Android 25.5.0 and Google UMP Android 4.0.0 resolved and present in the new non-development validation AAB, based on manifest/dependency and derived-APK code inspection. This establishes packaging for that artifact, not final-candidate or live network behavior.

LevelPlay 9.5.1, its adapter and Android hook were removed from source inputs. The handoff reports the old ironSource/LevelPlay/Unity Ads class packages, native dependencies and startup components absent from that validation artifact. Historical APKs still contain the old stack. No extra mediation networks were introduced. Google advertising-ID, app-set-ID and measurement support remain; this is not an identifier-free or measurement-free integration.

Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure describes collection and sharing of IP addresses (which may indicate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP contacts Google's consent/message services and processes consent-related state. The exact final configuration and provider disclosures must determine the app's final Data safety answers.

Watching ads is optional, but that does not establish that every SDK data flow is optional. Source inspection and simulated tests have not verified all startup traffic, actual identifier access, encrypted transport on every path, provider retention or international transfers. Delaying measurement initialization and checking ad permission in game code do not prove zero processing before consent.

Development Android builds and validation/test-inventory AABs use Google's official rewarded test unit; desktop TEST AD simulations are local tests. Ordinary production compilation selects the owner's rewarded unit, but production advertising remains blocked by an audience-review activation guard pending owner decisions and native-device validation. Test inventory is not evidence of zero personal-data processing. See Google's test ad guidance.

5. Relevant service providers

Google supplies the integrated advertising and consent services. Read how Google uses information from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy for its advertising, measurement, service operation and abuse-prevention practices and applicable choices.

The game uses Unity 6000.4.6f1 and Unity Mobile Notifications 2.4.3. Unity External Dependency Manager 2.1.0 resolves Editor/build dependencies; it is not a standalone player analytics service. The handoff reports no newly introduced standalone analytics/crash upload, developer backend or remote push service. These findings do not establish every native component's live processing.

Gmail processes support messages (section 7), and GitHub Pages hosts this website (section 14). Android backup or transfer may separately process eligible app files and SDK preferences. Review the final build, provider/account configuration and device evidence before approving the final recipient and processing disclosures.

6. Advertising consent and privacy choices

The implemented launch flow updates UMP consent information, presents a consent form when required, and checks Google's CanRequestAds() result before initializing, requesting or showing rewarded ads, subject also to the release activation guard. The game does not treat refusal as unrestricted permission for non-personalized ads. Partial choices and valid prior-session permission during errors remain SDK decisions; no game-owned consent cache replaces UMP.

In the game's Options, Ad privacy choices is visible and interactable when UMP requires a privacy-options entry point. It opens the SDK choices so players can revisit them. A privacy change invalidates cached and in-flight ads. Options also includes a browser link to this public policy. These controls are implemented in code; regional form presentation, conditional visibility, errors and the effect of changes still require Android phone testing with the final configuration. See Google's UMP guidance.

Ad failure, no-fill, offline conditions or closing an ad do not grant a reward. Rewards require the genuine client SDK reward callback, remain tied to their original saved destination and are granted at most once by the implemented transaction handling. There is no developer-operated server-side ad verification endpoint.

New Game and Restore defaults are separate from advertising privacy choices. They do not implement global deletion of Google or provider records. UMP and Google manage their own SDK preferences and network-side data; their exact storage and retention are not established by the handoff. Declining personalization may still involve other advertising processing. Android advertising-ID controls are additional device controls, not a substitute for applicable in-app choices.

8. Retention, backups and deletion

New Game resets progress; it is not complete deletion. It retains options and app-created backups, including a timestamped pre-reset copy. Ascension deliberately retains permanent progression, and Restore defaults changes settings. No comprehensive in-game Delete local data action has been implemented.

The recovery backup, migration copies and historical save/claim ledgers can remain until removed by app, operating-system or user actions. No age- or count-based backup pruning has been added and no fixed retention period is promised. Bounded retention and comprehensive local deletion remain planned work.

Android Clear storage, uninstall and operating-system backup controls are external controls. Their effects on retained or restored files depend on the device, OS, account and effective backup rules and still require testing. Do not assume uninstall erases every copy or that backup guarantees recovery. There is no developer-operated service for remotely accessing or erasing local saves.

Advertising privacy choices do not erase all provider-held information. Google's retention information explains its provider-controlled practices. Support-email retention criteria, request procedures and provider coordination remain developer decisions; immediate or worldwide erasure is not promised.

9. Security

Local saves are readable JSON. Their unkeyed SHA-256 corruption check is not encryption or protection against deliberate modification. Validation and atomic save replacement help with file integrity but are not a guarantee of security. Inspection of game code and a validation artifact has not verified every SDK network path or transmission protection. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Children and teenagers

The proposed audience includes ages 13–15, 16–17 and adults, with broad international availability. Final age groups, countries and treatment of users below the applicable consent age or of unknown age remain unresolved. There is no age screen, birth-date collection or verified-adult status in the current implementation.

Development defaults request teen advertising treatment and a T ad-content cap; UMP's separate under-consent flag is false by default. These settings do not establish that every regional teenage audience is correctly classified. Production advertising remains gated pending audience review. A minimum age of 13 is not a universal legal consent threshold or assurance of worldwide compliance. Review the relevant Google Play audience guidance, regional requirements and any required parental involvement before release activation.

13. Future production purchases

The closed test has no real-money purchasing or Google Play Billing. Earned diamonds can buy existing random manager chests and other game items. Release UI omits diamond-pack prices and paid checkout controls; development-only TEST FREE packs are test tools, not payments. No money is charged and no Play purchase token is handled.

Google Play Billing diamond packs, purchased diamonds usable for random manager chests, purchase verification, fulfilment, recovery and refunds are a separate future milestone. No real-purchase restoration guarantee exists today. Before payments are activated, review actual transaction processing, retention, audience and territory restrictions, purchase controls and indirect paid-random-reward disclosures. Existing odds/contents UI does not establish certified probabilities or legal approval for paid random rewards.
