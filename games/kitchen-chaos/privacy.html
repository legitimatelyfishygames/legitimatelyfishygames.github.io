1. About this policy

Legitimately Fishy Games develops Kitchen Chaos: Idle Cooking for Android through Google Play. This policy covers the first closed-test release, version 0.36.1, version code 43, package com.legitimatelyfishygames.kitchenchaos, prepared for submission. It does not describe planned commercial features as currently available.

The intended audience is adults aged 18 and over only. Closed-test territories are the United Kingdom, Poland, United States and New Zealand. This does not promise future public availability or a public release date.

The current build retains gameplay, local saves and local progression, including offline progression, managers and random manager chests using earned diamonds. Rewarded advertisements and real-money checkout are disabled. Native Google Mobile Ads and UMP, advertising permissions and active advertising/consent initialization paths are excluded. Google Play Billing 9 and its BILLING permission remain packaged, but no purchase connection occurs. Future advertising and purchases are described separately in sections 4, 6 and 13.

There is no player account system or proprietary cloud-save service. You do not create a studio account to play. Local saves, SDK processing, Android/Google Play services, support correspondence and this website have different information-handling practices; having no player account does not establish that no personal data is processed.

2. Locally stored game data

The game stores progress and settings locally on your device. Saves include production and recipes, currencies and spending, managers and assignments, chest inventory and results, rewards and claims, tutorials, and timestamps used for offline progress. Local identifiers and transaction records help prevent duplicate actions or rewards.

Save recovery files, migration copies and pre-reset backups can retain earlier progress. The game has a Settings → New game option; its limits are explained in section 8.

Android may separately back up or transfer eligible app files and preferences, depending on your operating system, device, account and settings. This is separate from studio cloud saves. We do not guarantee backup availability, recovery or deletion of Android-managed backups. See Android backup information.

3. Optional local notifications

Optional quest reminders use Unity Mobile Notifications and are off by default. If you enable them, Android may request notification permission. Reminders are scheduled locally; they do not require a studio push-notification server.

Reminder text may reveal game information on your lock screen. You can change notification preferences in the game and Android's app notification settings. Notification permission is separate from advertising consent.

4. Current SDK processing and future advertising

Current closed test — 0.36.1 (43): rewarded ads are disabled. Native Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), advertising permissions and active ad/consent initialization paths are excluded. This build does not offer rewarded ad viewing or an operational UMP consent flow.

Managed SDK code still reads some device and app properties locally. The build audit identified no active off-device transmission in the startup paths it examined. Complete absence of network traffic across the distributed build was not independently established. These findings do not support a blanket claim that no information is collected or transmitted, or that all remaining SDK code is inactive.

Android and Google Play may separately process store, device, security or diagnostic information under their settings and policies, including possible crash reports. This is distinct from asserting that this game sends crash logs to a particular SDK or studio service. See Google Play data use.

Planned later update: optional rewarded advertising may be introduced with appropriate notices and privacy controls. If Google Mobile Ads and UMP are introduced, the actual SDK versions and configuration will determine the data handled and consent requirements. Google's Mobile Ads disclosure describes advertising-related app interactions, diagnostics, device or other IDs, and IP-derived approximate location. This describes a possible future integration, not active advertising collection in build 43. We will update this policy before introducing those features.

5. Third-party services

Google Play distributes the game, and Android/Google services may provide platform diagnostics, backup or device transfer depending on settings. Google Play Billing 9 and the BILLING permission remain in the build, but checkout is disabled and no purchase connection occurs. Read the Google Privacy Policy. Native Google advertising and UMP services are excluded from this closed-test AAB.

The game is built with Unity and retains managed SDK code, including its Unity IAP purchase integration, and Unity Mobile Notifications for local reminders. Local device/app-property reads remain; the limited startup audit does not establish every component's behavior across all execution paths. Unity IAP processing depends on package version and enabled services. See Unity IAP privacy information and the Unity player privacy policy. Inclusion of an SDK or a provider-policy link is not proof of active transmission to that provider.

Support messages are handled through Gmail, and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Their processing is separate from playing the game. Local gameplay saves are not a studio-operated cloud-save database.

6. Current privacy choices and future consent controls

UMP consent is not operational in closed-test build 43. No advertising consent form or functioning UMP “Ad privacy choices” control is promised for this release. Advertising is disabled; a consent choice is not needed to unlock basic gameplay in this build.

You can manage local notification permissions through Android settings, manage device storage and backup settings, and contact the studio about information it holds. Settings → New game has the limits in section 8. These controls do not erase all platform or provider records.

Before advertising is introduced in a later update, we will update this policy and provide appropriate notices and applicable consent/privacy controls. If UMP is used, the release must support applicable messages, ad-request eligibility checks and a way to revisit privacy options when required. Where processing relies on consent, applicable controls must allow changing or withdrawing it. See Google's UMP guidance. This is a future-release requirement, not a description of the current no-ads build.

7. Emails and privacy enquiries

If you email mati4193.004@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any name, attachments or other information you include. We use this information to respond, provide support and handle privacy requests. Gmail processes the correspondence under Google's applicable policies.

8. Retention, reset and deletion

Settings → New game resets gameplay progress; it is not a complete personal-data deletion tool. Settings and recovery, migration or pre-reset save copies may remain on the device. Settings → New game does not delete Google's, Unity's or other providers' records, store purchase history, support emails or Android-managed backups.

Local saves and backups can remain until removed by app, device or user actions. Android's Clear storage and uninstall controls can remove app data from the device, but their effects on backups, transfers and later restoration depend on the operating system and settings. We do not promise that uninstalling deletes every copy or that resetting restores or refunds purchases.

To request deletion of support correspondence or other personal information held by the studio, contact the email in section 15. We can address information within our control, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions. We cannot remotely erase local saves through a proprietary cloud service because we do not operate one.

Third-party retention and deletion follow the relevant provider's practices and legal obligations, including Google's retention information. There is no single retention period or deletion action covering local files, support messages and any applicable provider records. Advertising and purchase records would require separate review if those features are introduced.

9. Security

Local saves are readable files, not an encrypted vault. Save validation and recovery mechanisms help with integrity and recovery, but do not guarantee protection against access or modification. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. International processing

Google, Unity, GitHub and their infrastructure may process information outside your country. Processing locations and applicable safeguards depend on the service and its arrangements; the closed-test country list is not a promise that data stays in those countries. See the providers' linked privacy policies, including Google's data-transfer information.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on it; or complain to a data-protection authority. Conditions and exceptions may apply.

Email mati4193.004@gmail.com with your request and the information concerned. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request. Requests concerning provider-controlled data may also need the provider's privacy controls or request process. A request does not guarantee deletion of data outside the studio's control.

12. Adults only (18+)

Kitchen Chaos is intended for adults aged 18 and over only, matching its Google Play target-audience setting. It is not directed at children or teenagers under 18. A target-audience setting is not verification of every player's age.

If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information to the studio, contact us so we can investigate and address information within our control. This does not promise that we can identify or erase all records held independently by providers.

13. Current earned diamonds and future paid purchases

Paid diamond packs are not available in this closed test. Players can use earned diamonds for random manager chests.

Once paid packs are enabled, purchased diamonds are planned to be usable for random manager chests. Applicable reward odds must be disclosed in advance of, and close to, purchases leading to randomized items. This policy does not certify odds or replace required in-game disclosures. Store transaction and fulfilment records may remain after Settings → New game when purchases exist. See Google Play's payments policy.
