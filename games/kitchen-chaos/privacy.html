How Kitchen Chaos: Idle Cooking handles information and the choices available to you.

1. About this policy

Legitimately Fishy Games provides Kitchen Chaos: Idle Cooking for Android through Google Play. This policy explains information handled by the game, its integrated services, support email and this studio website.

The Google Play target audience is adults aged 18 and over only. Closed testing currently targets the United Kingdom, Poland, United States and New Zealand. These are testing territories; they do not define or promise the territories of a future worldwide or public release.

The game includes local saves, offline progression, managers, random manager chests, optional rewarded advertising and optional local notifications. Google Play Billing through Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP) is integrated for six diamond packs; real-money checkout is currently disabled pending product configuration and testing. See section 13 for purchase processing and random rewards.

There is no player account system or proprietary cloud-save service. You do not create a studio account to play. Local saves and third-party advertising, store and platform processing are separate: having no player account does not mean that no personal data is processed.

2. Locally stored game data

The game stores progress and settings locally on your device. Saves include production and recipes, currencies and spending, managers and assignments, chest inventory and results, rewards and claims, tutorials, and timestamps used for offline progress. Local identifiers and transaction records help prevent duplicate actions or rewards.

Save recovery files, migration copies and pre-reset backups can retain earlier progress. The game has a Reset Progress option; its limits are explained in section 8.

Android may separately back up or transfer eligible app files and preferences, depending on your operating system, device, account and settings. This is separate from studio cloud saves. We do not guarantee backup availability, recovery or deletion of Android-managed backups. See Android backup information.

3. Optional local notifications

Optional quest reminders use Unity Mobile Notifications and are off by default. If you enable them, Android may request notification permission. Reminders are scheduled locally; they do not require a studio push-notification server.

Reminder text may reveal game information on your lock screen. You can change notification preferences in the game and Android's app notification settings. Notification permission is separate from advertising consent.

4. Advertising, diagnostics and SDK data

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) for rewarded advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent handling and applicable privacy choices. You can choose whether to watch a rewarded ad; basic play does not require watching one.

Google's Mobile Ads Unity data disclosure describes automatic collection and sharing for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Depending on settings and applicable choices, information handled through AdMob includes:

  • App interactions: launches, taps, ad interactions and video views.
  • Diagnostics: app and SDK performance information, such as launch times, hangs and energy use.
  • Device or other IDs: advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, account-related identifiers on the device.
  • Approximate location: the device's IP address can be used to estimate general location.

Crash logs may also be collected through platform diagnostics or SDK services to investigate errors and improve stability. Google describes crash reports among information processed through Google Play. This is separate from claiming that every crash log is collected or shared by AdMob.

Optional ad viewing does not mean all SDK processing is optional or starts only when you watch an ad. UMP also communicates with Google's consent services and stores consent-related choices. Changing advertising choices does not necessarily stop diagnostics, fraud prevention or all other processing.

5. Third-party services

Google provides advertising, consent and Google Play store services. Read the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps for its practices and controls.

The game is built with Unity and uses Unity IAP for its Google Play purchase integration and Unity Mobile Notifications for local reminders. Unity IAP data handling depends on the installed version and enabled services; disabled checkout alone does not establish that an SDK processes no information. See Unity IAP privacy and consent information and the Unity player privacy policy.

Support messages are handled through Gmail, and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Third parties handle information under their applicable policies and settings. Local gameplay saves are not a studio-operated cloud-save database.

6. Advertising consent and privacy choices

The game uses UMP to update consent information and show applicable privacy messages. The advertising flow checks the SDK's permission to request ads. The message and choices available depend on your region and the applicable Google configuration.

Open Options → Ad privacy choices to revisit the UMP privacy form when Google requires that entry point. Options also links to this policy. Where processing relies on consent, you can change or withdraw the relevant consent through the available privacy controls. Refusing personalized advertising is not a promise that all advertising-related processing stops. See Google's UMP guidance.

Android's advertising settings may also let you reset or delete your advertising ID; availability and labels vary by device and Android version. These controls do not erase all provider-held records. Resetting game progress is separate from advertising privacy choices.

7. Emails and privacy enquiries

If you email mati4193.004@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any name, attachments or other information you include. We use this information to respond, provide support and handle privacy requests. Gmail processes the correspondence under Google's applicable policies.

Please send only information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details. Support correspondence is separate from local game saves and advertising data.

8. Retention, reset and deletion

Reset Progress resets gameplay progress; it is not a complete personal-data deletion tool. Settings and recovery, migration or pre-reset save copies may remain on the device. Reset Progress does not delete Google's, Unity's or other providers' records, store purchase history, support emails or Android-managed backups.

Local saves and backups can remain until removed by app, device or user actions. Android's Clear storage and uninstall controls can remove app data from the device, but their effects on backups, transfers and later restoration depend on the operating system and settings. We do not promise that uninstalling deletes every copy or that resetting restores or refunds purchases.

To request deletion of support correspondence or other personal information held by the studio, contact the email in section 15. We can address information within our control, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions. We cannot remotely erase local saves through a proprietary cloud service because we do not operate one.

Third-party retention and deletion follow the relevant provider's practices and legal obligations, including Google's retention information. There is no single retention period or deletion action covering local files, support messages, advertising records and store transactions.

9. Security

Local saves are readable files, not an encrypted vault. Save validation and recovery mechanisms help with integrity and recovery, but do not guarantee protection against access or modification. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. International processing

Google, Unity, GitHub and their infrastructure may process information outside your country. Processing locations and applicable safeguards depend on the service and its arrangements; the closed-test country list is not a promise that data stays in those countries. See the providers' linked privacy policies, including Google's data-transfer information.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on it; or complain to a data-protection authority. Conditions and exceptions may apply.

Email mati4193.004@gmail.com with your request and the information concerned. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request. Requests concerning provider-controlled data may also need the provider's privacy controls or request process. A request does not guarantee deletion of data outside the studio's control.

12. Adults only (18+)

Kitchen Chaos is intended for adults aged 18 and over only, matching its Google Play target-audience setting. It is not directed at children or teenagers under 18. A target-audience setting is not verification of every player's age.

If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information to the studio, contact us so we can investigate and address information within our control. This does not promise that we can identify or erase all records held independently by providers.

13. Diamond purchases and random manager chests

Current purchase status (22 October 2026): Google Play Billing / Unity IAP is integrated for six diamond packs. Real-money checkout is currently disabled pending Google Play product configuration and testing. Integration does not mean paid purchases are currently available.

When purchases are enabled: optional diamond-pack payments are processed through Google Play using Unity IAP. Google handles payment details under its policies. The purchase integration handles purchase information such as product identifiers, transaction or order identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, and purchase status to process transactions, grant diamonds and prevent duplicate fulfilment. Store transaction records and local fulfilment records are separate from advertising choices and may remain after Reset Progress.

Players can spend diamonds on random manager chests. Once paid packs are enabled, purchased diamonds can also be used for those chests. Google Play requires reward odds to be clearly disclosed in advance of, and close to, purchases leading to randomized items. Applicable reward-odds disclosures must be available before paid purchases are activated. This policy does not certify the game's odds or replace the required in-game disclosure. See Google Play's payments policy.

14. This studio website

This static website has no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, third-party tracking widgets or tracking cookies installed by Legitimately Fishy Games. The policy is ordinary HTML and requires no login or cookie acceptance to read. Visiting this website does not load the game's advertising SDKs.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub documents logging and storage of visitor IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting and browser infrastructure may process visitor information even without studio-installed tracking. External links lead to services with their own practices.
